WHAT CHANGED, AND WHY
---------------------

1. The mouse feature is gone. All of it. You said to give up on it and
   I have: the raw mouse input, the waiting, the setting, the console
   command and the checkbox in the dialog. Any input now wakes
   keep-alive and nothing tries to work out what caused it.

   Two things follow from that. Waking is a quarter of a second quicker
   than it was in your last round, because the wait I added is gone. And
   brushing the trackpad will take the headset back from your phone -
   but switching keep-alive off by hand still stops that, exactly as
   before, so a deliberate handover is not undone by a stray finger.

2. The settings hotkey is off by default. Ctrl+Win+F11 belongs to
   everyone else again. There is a checkbox in the dialog, "Also use a
   hotkey to open these settings", with Ctrl+Win+F11 already filled in
   beside it, so switching it on is one press.

3. When a hotkey is refused, the message now says which mistake you
   made and then how to write one properly - every spelling of every
   modifier, every key name it will take, and the fact that the order
   does not matter. It never did, and now it says so.

4. The wording. "Let go of them" is now "Let go of the headphones", and
   I went through every other label the same way. Nothing in the dialog
   should now need you to have heard something earlier to make sense.

5. The group headings. I could not do this one, and I want to be
   straight about it rather than have you hunt for a change that is not
   there. I found no way from inside the app to make JAWS announce
   them. The obvious thing to try turned out to be already switched on,
   and the other thing I tried made no difference either. Test 35 asks
   you to look at one JAWS setting, because that is now my best guess.

6. Logging - my steer, which you asked for. I have split it rather than
   choosing. The ordinary log stays on: it is one line per state change,
   a few hundred bytes a day, and it is the only evidence either of us
   has when something goes wrong, because this app's behaviour cannot be
   watched while it happens. Every round of yours that produced a real
   fix instead of a guess produced it from that file.

   The detailed log stays off until something is being looked into, and
   it now records what the app hears - when audio starts and stops, and
   how loud - which is the thing neither of us can observe any other
   way. The checkbox is "Detailed logging, for troubleshooting".

7. The app now has a Windows application manifest, which it turns out
   it never had. Without one, the dialog was built from an older
   generation of Windows controls than every other dialog on your
   machine. It should be neutral or slightly better. Test 34 checks it
   did not disturb anything, since the dialog passed your last round on
   the old ones.

Not done, on purpose, and both waiting for Milestone 6: the help file
with a button to open it, and removing the console window.
